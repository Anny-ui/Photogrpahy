Good to know

Questions, answered.

How far ahead should we book?

For weddings, we recommend booking as early as your dates are locked. For smaller events or portraits in Hyderabad, 1–2 weeks notice is usually fine. Message us to check availability.

Do you travel outside Hyderabad?

Yes, absolutely. We are based in Hyderabad, but regularly travel for destination weddings and devotional shoots across India. Travel and stay are discussed upfront with no hidden costs.

How quickly do we receive our pictures?

We provide a curated preview within 48–72 hours of your event, and the complete high-resolution gallery following full color grading.

Do you cover traditional rituals and devotional events?

Yes. We have deep respect for Indian wedding customs, Shiv Aaradhana, Mata Rani Jagran, and Bhajan & Kirtan gatherings. We shoot unobtrusively, avoiding harsh flash whenever required.
